### The 5 Headline Findings

1. **Label corruption is catastrophically destructive**: ρ drops to 0.28 (Breast Cancer + LogReg), while Gaussian noise/feature dropout keep ρ > 0.95
2. **Rank stability ≠ magnitude stability**: XGBoost ρ=0.96 but L=7.67 — a novel distinction
3. **EBM is the most stable model**: 2.2× better than LogReg on Breast Cancer
4. **Decision Trees are surprisingly unstable**: "inherently interpretable" ≠ stable
5. **SHAP fidelity can go negative**: Explanations become actively misleading under label corruption
